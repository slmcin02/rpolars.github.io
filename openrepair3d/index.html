Case 001 — Dryer switch mount failure

A clothes dryer remains mechanically and electrically serviceable and the electrical switch itself is new, but a small molded mounting feature in the dryer structure has broken. That small failure can force replacement of a much larger assembly or make an otherwise working appliance impractical to repair.

The OpenRepair3D workflow for Case 001 is straightforward: document the failed geometry, measure intact reference features, design a mechanically secure replacement or retainer, select an appropriate material for the environment, print and fit-test revisions, verify clearance from electrical terminals and moving parts, then document the final design and its durability.

Safety note: printed components are not used as electrical insulation, current-carrying parts, or substitutes for required safety devices. Repairs involving mains-powered equipment are validated with the appliance disconnected and retain required electrical clearances and protections.

What each published repair should contain

Evidence

  • Failure description and photographs
  • Key physical measurements
  • Original-vs-replacement cost context

Design

  • CAD revision history
  • STEP/STL/3MF where appropriate
  • Material-selection rationale
  • Print orientation and settings

Validation

  • Dimensional checks
  • Fit/function observations
  • Relevant heat/load/vibration considerations
  • 30/90-day follow-up on selected parts

AI-assisted reconstruction, human-validated

The longer-term workflow combines calibrated photographs with direct physical measurements to help reconstruct candidate replacement geometry. AI may assist with image organization, measurement reconciliation, design alternatives, documentation, and test planning; final dimensions, CAD, safety decisions, and physical validation remain human-controlled.

Hardware support sought

The project is seeking an engineering-capable FDM platform suitable for functional repair work, ideally with reliable automatic calibration and support for materials such as PLA, PETG, ASA, and TPU where appropriate. Hardened nozzle capability, spare hotend/nozzle parts, build surfaces, and starter materials would enable a broader validation matrix.

Evaluation support is welcome from manufacturers, beta programs, product-seeding programs, demo/refurbished inventory programs, or project sponsors. Feedback will be candid; hardware support does not guarantee a positive review.

Complete support matrix

OpenRepair3D can put more than a printer to productive use. Support can be a full package or a single component, and safely functional demo, open-box, refurbished, returned, previous-generation, engineering-sample, or cosmetically imperfect equipment is welcome.

Fabrication platform

  • Enclosed or engineering-capable FDM printer
  • Multi-material/tool-changing system where available
  • Automatic calibration and reliable monitoring
  • High-temperature and abrasive-material capability where supported

Materials

  • PLA / PLA+
  • PETG
  • ASA
  • TPU / flexible materials
  • Nylon, CF/GF composites, and engineering polymers when machine-compatible

Drying & storage

  • Active filament dryer
  • Multi-spool dry storage
  • Vacuum bags / sealed storage
  • Desiccant and humidity monitoring

Wear parts & tooling

  • Hardened and standard nozzles
  • Spare hotends / heaters / thermistors
  • Extruder and feed-path components
  • Textured and smooth build plates
  • Adhesives, maintenance parts, and service tools

Measurement & digitization

  • Calipers and precision measurement tools
  • 3D scanner / photogrammetry hardware
  • Calibration targets and inspection tools

Safety & workflow

  • Enclosure / filtration / extraction where applicable
  • Printer camera / monitoring
  • Spare consumables for sustained testing
  • Software or workflow support relevant to CAD, slicing, or validation

Sponsors may be acknowledged transparently on relevant project documentation. Sponsorship never buys a positive result: test observations, limitations, failures, and successful outcomes are documented candidly.
