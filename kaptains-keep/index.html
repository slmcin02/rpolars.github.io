Kaptains Keep

Built to be kept.

Custom engraved keepsakes, premium boxes, personalized gifts, and small-batch maker goods with the grit of old American tattoo flash and the soul of a sailor's workshop.

The idea

Kaptains Keep is for things that should outlive the moment they were bought for. We take wood, metal, leather, boxes, tags, and everyday objects and turn them into pieces with names, stories, milestones, and character.

Make it personal. Make it useful. Make it worth keeping.

What we make

Engraved Keepsakes

Names, dates, coordinates, memorials, milestones, family pieces, and gifts that carry a story.

Premium Boxes

Refinished cigar boxes, display boxes, memory boxes, inserts, upgraded hardware, and custom interiors.

Laser + Maker Goods

Wood, acrylic, leather, metal marking, tags, signs, jigs, organizers, small-batch accessories, and oddball one-offs.

Custom Requests

If it sounds a little strange, sentimental, overbuilt, or hard to find in a store, that's probably our lane.

Brand DNA

Look

American traditional tattoo flash, nautical stars, anchors, rope, swallows, banners, black ink, oxblood red, deep navy, warm gold, cream paper, and worn wood.

Voice

Crafted, confident, rough around the edges, funny when it fits, never fake-luxury corporate.

Promise

If it carries the Kaptains Keep name, it should feel intentional, useful, personal, and worth hanging onto.

Follow from day one

We're building the workshop, product line, laser setup, packaging, and every ugly prototype in public. The wins stay. The screwups stay too.
